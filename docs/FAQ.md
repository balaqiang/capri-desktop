# FAQ

**She can't be clicked, or she's behind my windows.**
She puts herself back on top every few seconds; if a click still goes through, move the mouse a little (Windows tells her
window about the pointer only when it moves).

**A black screen behind her.**
Some GPUs (NVIDIA with FXAA, MFAA or HDR) draw her see-through window black: her menu › **Display** › **OpenGL**, or
**Without the graphics card**.

**She barely moves.**
Windows animations are off (Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects): her menu › **Animations** › **Full**. Or she
learned you don't like her moving: say "boleh gerak lagi".

**She typed nothing, or stopped halfway.**
She stops when you switch windows, press a key, or the box is a password box; she says which. Name the app if she picked
the wrong window ("type hello in notepad").

**She typed what I said instead of writing it.**
"Type …" types your words as they are; "write an email…", "write simple html here", "buatkan surat…" have her write it.

**Her math is wrong.**
Sums are worked out by her calculator; a word problem is still her model's reasoning. A bigger model (gemma3, Gemini,
GPT-4o) reasons better than a small one.

**Look says something wrong.**
Use a model that can see (gemma3, Gemini, GPT-4o); small text-only models can't.

**Spotify only searches.**
Keep the Spotify app open; she presses Play through Windows' accessibility interface.

**No lyrics over her head.**
LRCLIB doesn't have every song, and has some only without timing (she still knows those). She hides them while she talks,
works or sleeps. The player has to show in Windows' volume flyout.

**A plugin doesn't go off.**
Check it's switched on, and its **Not again for**. A time plugin goes off at that minute while she's running; an app
plugin when the app comes to the front (not while it already is).

**Mac, Linux, phone?**
Windows is what she's made for; from the source she may start elsewhere, without the Windows tricks.

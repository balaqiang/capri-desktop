# Commands she understands

Say them or type them, in English or Indonesian; no AI needed for these. Her name and a polite start are fine
("Hello Capri, can you…", "tolong…"), and so is joining a few with "and", "lalu", "terus" ("open notepad and type
hello"). Said in other words, her model works out which command you meant ([in your own words](FEATURES.md#smarter-commands)).

## Hotkeys

| Hotkey | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+Alt+C | Put her away / bring her back, hop off your cursor, stop a glitch |
| Ctrl+Alt+T | Her chat |
| Ctrl+Alt+V | Talk to her (microphone) |
| Ctrl+Alt+L | She looks at your screen |
| Ctrl+Alt+S | Her settings |
| Esc (while she types) | Stop typing |

All of them can be changed in Settings › Advanced.
